## Maglev Vehicle

Built a small magnetic-levitation vehicle to demonstrate the basic principles of a maglev system. I arranged and tested the magnets by hand, experimented with several track and guidance designs, and eventually used a monorail to keep the vehicle stable while still allowing it to levitate. A small fan provided enough thrust for the vehicle to move along the track.

## Catapult

Built a wooden catapult from scratch using a rotating launch arm mounted on an axle. I added an adjustable stopper to control the arm's release angle and a pin-and-chain mechanism to hold and release the arm before launch.
